English

GlibberJagd collects no personal data: no account, no analytics, no ads, no in-app purchases, no server of its own. The camera image (room, hand and road detection) and the motion sensors (counting exercises) are processed on device only while in use and are never stored or sent. First names and favourite monsters in party mode, history, scores and aliens built in the lab stay on the device. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. Contact: deepframe.support@gmail.com.
